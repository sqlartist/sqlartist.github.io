Bah, Hum-bug!

The AI Scrooge cartoon. Three minutes, seven scenes, about 2,100 frames. Every character is a puppet made of SQL Server geometry parts, every move is a row in a keyframe table, and every frame is drawn by a stored procedure.

Tick Show the SQL on any scene to see what the renderer is doing for the frame on screen: the call, the keyframes in play, and a row of the geometry it produced.

Track AI Scrooge live, every day of December →
Made by Trevor Dwyer, with Claude. Rendered with the toon schema in Spatial Studio for SQL Server 2025: sx.Affine moves the shapes, a recursive CTE composes the puppets, and sx.Jitter adds the felt-tip wobble. The Earth in scene 5 is Natural Earth (public domain). Bah, hum-bug.
