what: "A space trading game by two Cambridge students, Ian Bell and David Braben, first published on the BBC Micro. It held eight galaxies of 256 planets each, all generated from a few numbers, in a machine with 32K of memory."
---

Nobody had seen anything like it: an open universe in wireframe 3D where you could trade, fight or turn pirate. It's one of the most influential games ever made. My friends Gordon and Gareth lived in it while my Commodore 16 sulked. Underneath, as I found out forty years later, it's a database wearing a spaceship costume.
